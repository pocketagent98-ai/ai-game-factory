# ART_STYLE — Merge & Bloom

## Look
Bright, colourful, cohesive 2D with soft shading and chunky readable shapes. Each tier visibly “levels up” in beauty — the reward signal. Cozy magical-garden mood.

## Palette
- Background: deep garden green gradient (`#0f3d2e` → `#124b39`).
- UI panels: `#173e33` with light hairline borders.
- Accent: mint `#7ef0b0`; gold `#ffd54f` for coins.
- Tier colours: a distinct hue per tier (green → pink → purple → orange → gold → teal).

## Tiers (glyphs used in v1; replace with art later)
1 🌱 Sprout · 2 🌿 Seedling · 3 🌷 Bud · 4 🌸 Bloom · 5 🫐 Berry Bush · 6 🍎 Fruit Tree · 7 🍇 Glow Vine · 8 💎 Crystal Bloom · 9 🌳 Golden Tree · 10 🌻 Sunflower Crown · 11 ✨ Aurora Tree · 12 🌍 World Tree

## Rules
- **Colourblind-safe:** tiers must differ by **shape/pattern**, not colour alone.
- Rewarded buttons: clear video icon, **never green**, never trick-placed.
- Thumbnails: bright, **text-free**, show a satisfying merge moment + the World Tree (the #1 CTR lever on web).
- VFX: merge particle burst, floating “+coins”, Bloom Meter glow. Watch transparent overdraw on mobile.

## Audio
Short rising chimes per tier; soft ambient garden loop; a satisfying merge pop. Small files (Vorbis/Opus, mono). Respect platform mute during ads.

## Asset rules
Original or CC0/MIT only. No watermarks, no misused IP. Bundle all assets (no external requests — portals block them).
